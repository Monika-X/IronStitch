1. Definitions

In these Terms, "IronSmith", "we", "us" and "our" refer to IronSmith Cobblers Ltd. "Client", "you" and "your" refer to the individual or entity leaving shoes for repair or restoration. "Goods" means the footwear or accessories left in our care. "Services" means the repair, restoration, cleaning or other work we carry out.

2. Our Services

We offer professional shoe repair and restoration services including but not limited to: sole replacement, heel repair, leather conditioning and polishing, stitching repair, waterproofing treatment, stretch fitting and bespoke insole production.

All services are subject to assessment. We reserve the right to decline any repair that we believe cannot be satisfactorily completed, would present a safety risk, or involves materials we cannot responsibly work with.

3. Quotes & Authorisation

3.1 Preliminary Assessment

We provide a free preliminary assessment. Any quote given verbally or in writing is an estimate unless explicitly stated as a fixed price. Final pricing may vary following detailed inspection.

3.2 Written Authorisation

No work commences without your explicit authorisation — verbal in-store, by email, or via our online form. By authorising work, you confirm ownership of the goods and your right to instruct repairs.

3.3 Amended Quotes

If during work we discover additional damage or complications requiring a revised quote, we will contact you before proceeding. You may accept the revised quote, decline (you will not be charged for work already completed as exploratory), or request a partial repair.

4. Payment Terms

  • Payment is due upon collection of your shoes.
  • We accept all major debit and credit cards, contactless, Apple Pay and Google Pay.
  • Bank transfer available for orders exceeding £500 (prior arrangement required).
  • We do not accept cheques.
  • For postal clients, payment is required before we dispatch your shoes back to you.
  • Unpaid items may be subject to a storage charge of £5 per week after 30 days.

5. Turnaround Times

Stated turnaround times are estimates. We will always contact you if a repair will be delayed beyond the quoted time. Busy periods (pre-Christmas, following bank holidays) may affect standard turnaround. Express services are available for most repairs at a premium of 40% above the standard price.

  • Same-day: Selected tip replacements and minor repairs (drop off by 11am)
  • 24–48 hours: Standard heel repairs, polishing and conditioning
  • 3–5 days: Sole replacements, colour restoration
  • 10–14 days: Heritage restorations and full reconstruction

6. Collection & Uncollected Items

We will notify you by phone and email when your shoes are ready. If you have not collected within 30 days of notification, we reserve the right to charge a storage fee of £5 per week. After 90 days of non-collection and repeated notification, we reserve the right to dispose of uncollected items, though we will make every reasonable effort to contact you first.

7. Liability & Limitation

7.1 Pre-existing Damage

We document the condition of all shoes received. We are not liable for pre-existing damage that is not reasonably apparent at the time of assessment, or for damage resulting from the natural condition of aged or heavily-worn leather.

7.2 Exotic and Fragile Materials

We will advise you if we have concerns about working with exotic skins (crocodile, ostrich, snake) or other fragile materials. Work on such materials is undertaken at the client's risk, and we will notify you of this in writing before commencing.

7.3 Maximum Liability

Our maximum liability for loss or damage to goods while in our care is limited to the reasonable market value of the goods at the time of deposit, not their original purchase price. We strongly recommend informing us of high-value items so additional precautions can be taken.

8. Workmanship Guarantee

We guarantee all repair work against failure due to defective materials or workmanship for a period of 12 months from collection, except where a different period is specified (e.g., lifetime guarantee on Heritage Restoration packages). This guarantee does not cover:

  • Damage from improper care after collection
  • Normal wear and tear
  • Damage caused by water, salt, chemicals or extreme conditions
  • Work carried out by third parties after collection

To claim under our guarantee, return the shoes with your original receipt. We will carry out remedial work free of charge if the failure falls within the guarantee terms.

9. Postal & Courier Service

For shoes sent to us by post, we do not accept liability for damage or loss during transit to IronSmith. We strongly recommend using a tracked, insured service. For shoes returned to you, we use insured, tracked courier services and are liable for loss or damage in transit up to the declared value.

The £15 concierge collection and delivery fee is non-refundable once the courier has been dispatched.
