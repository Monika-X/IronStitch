Quick Answers

Walk-ins are always welcome during opening hours. However, for heritage restorations or complex repairs, booking ahead ensures our senior craftspeople are available to assess your shoes immediately.
Turnaround varies by service: tip replacements same day, standard repairs 24–48 hours, sole replacements 3–5 days, heritage restorations 10–14 days. We'll confirm your timeline at assessment and never overrun without calling first.
