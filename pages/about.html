75+ Years

Our Founding Story

Born from Necessity,
Refined by Passion

In the post-war austerity of 1947, Edmund Ironsmith — a returning soldier with a craftsman's eye — opened a tiny workshop on Jermyn Street. His principle was simple: no shoe that could be saved should be discarded.

Within a decade, Edmund's reputation had spread through St James's gentlemen's clubs, Mayfair drawing rooms and the corridors of Westminster. He counted among his clients several members of the House of Lords and not a few actors from the West End stage.

His son Robert, and now his granddaughter Clara — IronStitch's current Master Cobbler — have preserved every principle Edmund established, while embracing the finest modern materials and techniques.

Our Journey

Seven Decades of Excellence

Every year has added new skills, new clients, and new chapters to the IronStitch story.

1947

The Beginning

Edmund Ironsmith opens a 12-foot workshop at 14 Jermyn Street with a single bench, two lasts and a lifetime of skill.

1962

Royal Warrant

IronStitch granted a Royal Warrant for services to the Royal Household — a distinction renewed every five years thereafter.

1981

Second Generation

Robert Ironsmith joins his father full-time after completing his master cobbler apprenticeship in Northampton, England's boot-making capital.

1998

Atelier Expansion

The workshop doubles in size, adding a dedicated leather restoration suite and accepting bespoke insole commissions.

2019

Third Generation

Clara Ironsmith — trained in Northampton, Paris and Florence — becomes Master Cobbler, blending heritage with contemporary luxury sensibility.

2024

18,500 Restorations

IronStitch celebrates a landmark — 18,500 pairs of shoes breathed new life, with zero compromises on quality in 77 years.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

Heritage Integrity

We use only techniques that honour the original maker's intention. Every stitch, welt and wax is chosen to match, not mask, the shoe's character.

Sustainable Luxury

Restoration is the ultimate sustainable act. We champion repair over replacement and source materials from British and European ethical suppliers.

Obsessive Precision

We measure twice, cut once. Each repair undergoes three-stage quality inspection before it leaves the workshop — no exceptions, no shortcuts.

Client Trust

You entrust us with shoes that often carry deep personal meaning. We take that responsibility seriously — and our 12-month guarantee reflects it.
