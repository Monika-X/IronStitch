1. Who We Are

IronStitch Cobblers Ltd ("IronStitch", "we", "our", "us") is a company registered in England and Wales. Registered office: 14 Jermyn Street, St. James's, London SW1Y 6LX. Company number: 12345678. VAT number: GB 987 6543 21.

We are the Data Controller for all personal data processed in connection with our cobblery and shoe restoration services. Our designated data contact is Clara Ironsmith, who can be reached at privacy@ironstitch.com.

2. Data We Collect

We collect personal data in the following circumstances:

2.1 When you make an enquiry or book a repair

  • Full name and contact details (email, phone, address)
  • Description of shoes and repair requirements
  • Service preferences and urgency
  • Communication history with our team

2.2 When you visit our website

  • IP address and device type (via analytics cookies, only with consent)
  • Pages visited and time spent (aggregated, anonymised)
  • Referring website or search terms

2.3 When you subscribe to our newsletter

  • Email address and first name
  • Email open and click activity (aggregated statistics only)

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data to:

  • Respond to your repair enquiries and provide quotes
  • Carry out and manage your shoe repair or restoration
  • Arrange collection, delivery or postal services
  • Process payments and issue receipts
  • Send transactional communications (repair updates, collection notifications)
  • Send our Journal newsletter if you have subscribed
  • Comply with legal obligations (accounting, tax, fraud prevention)
  • Improve our website and service quality

4. Legal Basis for Processing

  • Contractual necessity: Processing required to fulfil your repair service contract
  • Legitimate interests: Improving our services, fraud prevention, client communications
  • Consent: Newsletter subscription, analytics cookies (you may withdraw consent at any time)
  • Legal obligation: Tax records, accounting, regulatory compliance

5. Data Sharing

We never sell your personal data. We may share it only with:

  • Courier partners: For collection/delivery — only your name, address and contact number
  • Email platform (Mailchimp): For newsletter subscribers only — email and first name
  • Payment processors: For payment processing — we do not store card details
  • Accountants and HMRC: As required by law for tax purposes

All third-party processors are bound by Data Processing Agreements and comply with UK GDPR.

6. Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses the following categories of cookies:

  • Essential cookies: Session management, security — always active
  • Preference cookies: Dark/light mode, RTL preference — always active (stored locally only)
  • Analytics cookies (Google Analytics 4): Visitor statistics — only with your consent

You may manage your cookie preferences at any time by clicking "Cookie Settings" in our footer or clearing your browser cookies. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

7. Data Retention

  • Repair records: 7 years (UK accounting legal requirement)
  • Enquiry data (no order placed): 12 months
  • Newsletter subscriptions: Until unsubscription or 3 years of inactivity
  • Website analytics: 26 months (aggregated, anonymised)

8. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure: Request deletion ("right to be forgotten") where no legal obligation to retain
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Restriction: Limit how we use your data in certain circumstances
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent: For newsletter and analytics at any time, without affecting prior processing

To exercise any right, email privacy@ironstitch.com. We will respond within 30 days. No fee is charged for standard requests.

9. Security Measures

We take the security of your personal data seriously. Our measures include:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for all website data in transit
  • Encrypted, password-protected client record database
  • Access limited to authorised staff only
  • Annual security review and staff data protection training
  • Physical security at our Jermyn Street premises

10. Contact & Complaints

For all privacy enquiries: privacy@ironstitch.com or write to IronStitch Cobblers Ltd, 14 Jermyn Street, London SW1Y 6LX.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or by calling 0303 123 1113.

This policy is reviewed annually. Last reviewed by Clara Ironsmith, Master Cobbler & Director, November 2024.
