Service 06

Stretch Fitting

from £35 per pair

Our podiatrist-trained specialist uses precision mechanical lasts and controlled steam to expand specific areas of the shoe without damaging the leather's structure. Ideal for bunion relief, wide toe boxes and tight heels.

  • ✓ Targeted stretching — toe, ball, instep or heel
  • ✓ Up to 3/4 size length expansion
  • ✓ Works on all leather and most synthetic uppers
  • ✓ 24h overnight service included
