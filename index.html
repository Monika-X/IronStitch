Our Philosophy

Craftsmanship
is a Sacred Art

At IronStitch, we believe every pair of shoes tells a story. Our master cobblers — trained in the tradition of London's great ateliers — breathe new life into beloved footwear with meticulous attention to detail.

From the finest bench-made Oxfords to cherished everyday boots, we treat each pair with the same reverence and skill. Using only the highest-grade leathers, waxed threads, and traditional adhesives, our restorations often outlast the original construction.

What We Restore

Six signature services, each performed by a dedicated master craftsman using time-honoured techniques.

Sole Replacement

Complete sole reconstruction using Goodyear welt, Blake stitch, or cemented construction — matched to your shoe's original build.

Learn More

Heel Repair

Stiletto tip replacements, full heel rebuilds, and rubber top-piece fitting — precision-matched to original heel height and profile.

Learn More

Leather Polishing

Museum-quality finishing with Saphir Médaille d'Or waxes and hand-burnishing techniques that restore deep lustre to the leather.

Learn More

Stitching Repair

Hand-sewn repairs using waxed linen and braided nylon threads, perfectly colour-matched to the original stitching pattern.

Waterproofing

Professional-grade barrier treatments using Collonil Wax and Nano Protection — invisible shield against rain, salt and moisture.

Stretch Fitting

Precision mechanical and steam stretching to relieve tight spots, accommodate bunions, or widen narrow last fits — painlessly.
